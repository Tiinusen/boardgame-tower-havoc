# Light field cannon as first-edition weapon

This artifact records the operative Tower Havoc state for this bounded subject.

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Edition Rule ID: edition.weapon
- Decision: Dieselpunk Edition represents attacks with a light field cannon. The cannon is a thematic/readability component and does not physically fire a projectile; the D6 resolves the shot.

## Basis

Direct-fire artillery fits the side-wall hit metaphor better than a high-arc mortar and avoids requiring physical projectile safety/precision.

## Consequences

Barrel length, wheelbase, and firing arc currently carry no gameplay geometry and should remain cosmetic.
