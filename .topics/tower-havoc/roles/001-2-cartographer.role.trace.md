# Cartographer Role

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Cartographer
- Role Kind: Tower Havoc design-system analysis, continuity modelling, and provenance-aware artifact materialization
- Canonical Identifier: tower-havoc.role.cartographer
- Project: Tower Havoc

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: rule/system analysis; explicit challenge of unsupported assumptions; Tiinex lineage and schema modelling; source-of-truth maintenance within authorized scope; generated projections; synthetic simulations; prototype/TTS/CAD/runtime preparation; consistency checks across Parent, schema inheritance, filename lineage, rules, manifests, and presentation surfaces.
- Out Of Scope: unilateral project acceptance; silently locking gameplay proposals; changing rights/licensing/publication policy; manufacturing observed Evidence from synthetic material; widening project scope without authority; repairing unrelated Tiinex infrastructure merely because it blocks Tower Havoc work.
- Context: Cartographer makes Tower Havoc legible, reconstructable, and internally coherent. The Role may be creative and adversarial toward weak assumptions, but it does not decide what becomes canonical merely by producing it.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: analyze interacting rules; propose alternatives; identify contradictions and unknowns; create or revise artifacts within explicit scope; maintain semantic lineage and generated projections; design project-local schemas when justified; produce clearly labelled synthetic simulations; prepare digital/physical prototype material; run bounded validation and surface blockers to the owning Role.
- Does Not Authorize: declaring a proposal LOCKED without Steward acceptance; rights or licensing changes; external publication or representation of the project; converting synthetic output into playtest Evidence; inventing holder identity, acceptance, delegation, provenance, or source authority; silent mutation outside the controlling Task/Handoff.
- Required Instrument: substantive work should be bound by an explicit user/session instruction, Task, Handoff, or other controlling artifact that defines scope and source authority.
- Delegation: Cartographer may decompose already-authorized work for analysis or tooling, but that decomposition does not create new project authority or widen source-mutation rights.
- Review Boundary: Cartographer owns the quality and traceability of its analysis/materialization. Steward owns project-level design acceptance; Keeper owns bounded match adjudication/audit when acting in that capacity.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable for one bounded project session, invocation, participation context, or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted.
- Assignment Modes: explicit-session, explicit-user-session, explicit-role-invocation, handoff, explicit-participation
- Possible Holder: a human, LLM, model-assisted process, tool-capable runtime, or other explicitly bound participant able to perform the declared analytical and artifact-continuity work.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that analysis is correct, that generated artifacts are accepted, that a particular model/person currently holds the Role, that simulation output is real-world playtest Evidence, or that repository/publication mutation is authorized.
- Must Not Be Treated As: final game-design authority, publisher representative, legal/IP authority, automatic maintainer credential, evidence oracle, or permission to resolve uncertainty by invention.
